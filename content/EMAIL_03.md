## Email Body

Hi [First Name],

You've been on the waitlist for two weeks now.

By this point, you know what OneFoundr is. You've seen how the pod system works. You understand the AI co-founder is not another chatbot.

So let's talk about what it costs — and more importantly, what you're actually investing in.

### Why we're telling you now:

Most platforms reveal pricing at the point of conversion — when you're already committed and switching feels like friction. We're not doing that.

You should know what this costs before the cohort opens, so the decision you make is a considered one. Not a pressured one.

### The honest version of our pricing philosophy:

We built multiple tiers because solo founders are not all in the same place. What a side hustle founder needs — and what they can reasonably invest — is different from what a Stage 3 founder scaling past $100k needs.

We priced each tier to be the least expensive version of the support it replaces.

One bad hire costs more than a year of Core. One wasted quarter costs more than a year of Premium. One major strategic mistake — made alone, without the right sounding board — costs more than all of it.

That is not a sales argument. It's the actual maths of why structure around decision-making pays for itself.

### The question worth sitting with before you read the tiers:

**What is one better decision worth to your business over the next twelve months?**

Answer that honestly. Then read what follows through that lens.

## Here is what each tier includes:

### Free — $0/month
For founders who want to stay close to the OneFoundr world before committing.

- Weekly newsletter
- Founder content and resources
- Access to #founder-lounge community channel
- #resources channel

No pod access. No AI co-founder. But a real starting point.

### Starter — $25/month or $199/year
For founders who want structured accountability without live sessions.

- Async pod access — written check-ins with your matched group
- Weekly AI summaries of your pod activity
- Full Resource Hub access
- Weekly written accountability check-ins

**Best for:** Side hustle founders who need structure but have limited time for live sessions.

### Core — $49/month or $399/year
The full OneFoundr experience. This is where most founders start and stay.

- Everything in Starter
- Bi-weekly live pod sessions with your matched group
- Full AI co-founder access — unlimited, knows your business
- Decision Sprints — structured five-day async exercises that crack one real business decision
- Accountability tracking across sessions

**Best for:** Solopreneurs and early solo founders who are serious about building a consistent decision-making structure.

### Premium — $149/month or $999/year
For Stage 3 founders who need the highest level of peer challenge and visibility.

- Everything in Core
- Mastermind-tier pods — Stage 3 founders only, higher stakes, deeper challenge
- Advanced AI business health snapshots — pattern recognition across your decisions and progress over time
- OneFoundr Spotlight eligibility — featured in our newsletter and on the website

**Best for:** Solo founders scaling past $100k who need peer challenge at their level, not generic accountability.

### Accelerator — $167/month or $2,000/year
For the founder who wants direct access to strategic guidance alongside the pod system.

- Everything in Core
- Monthly 30-minute 1:1 strategy call with OneFoundr's strategy lead
- Quarterly 60-minute business review — a deep dive on your specific business
- Priority pod matching — you are matched first in every cohort

This tier has a hard cap. Twenty members per cohort. When it's full, it's full.

**Best for:** Founders at an inflection point — a major pivot, a pricing restructure, a first hire decision — who need structured strategic input alongside peer accountability.

### Alumni — $500/month — Invitation Only
For Stage 3 founders who have graduated through the pod system and want to remain inside it — as mentors, peers, and contributors.

- Mentor role — facilitate one pod session per quarter for Stage 2 pods
- Exclusive alumni Slack channel — a peer network of founders who have built through the system
- Deal flow and partnership opportunities shared within the alumni network
- OneFoundr Spotlight — featured in newsletter and on the website
- Input on product roadmap — quarterly strategy session with OneFoundr

This tier is not applied for. It is offered. If you reach Stage 3 inside OneFoundr and you've built something real, we'll come to you.
